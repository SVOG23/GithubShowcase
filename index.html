Vouchlist

Live on iOS & Android ALPHABYTE

Local recommendations from people you actually know.

vouchlist.app↗ Code kept private

Vouchlist replaces the anonymous five-star review with a recommendation from someone in your circle. You find a plumber, a restaurant or a place to travel because a real person you know stood behind it. I designed and built the whole product and took it through both app store reviews.

The problem What came of it

Online reviews are anonymous and easy to fake, so it's hard to know who is actually behind a rating. Meanwhile the people whose taste you genuinely trust (friends, neighbours, family) aren't the ones writing them.

Live on the App Store and Google Play, covering home services, restaurants and travel. Friends are matched privately, so people can find who they know without handing over their contacts.

Founder: design, product and engineering

TypeScript · React Native · Supabase · PostgreSQL

ModelMarketplace

iStartup Lab Micro-Grant ALPHABYTE

A marketplace where developers sell the machine-learning models they have trained.

Code kept private

Training a good model takes real time and skill, but there's no obvious place to sell one. ModelMarketplace gives developers somewhere to list their work and gives buyers a trustworthy way to pay for it.

The problem What came of it

Developers who train genuinely useful models have nowhere natural to sell them, and buyers have no safe way to pay for something they can't inspect first.

Three ways to buy: outright, by subscription, or by bidding at auction. Payments, seller payouts and refunds are all handled automatically. The University of Washington backed it with an iStartup Lab Micro-Grant for market viability and engineering quality.

Founder: product and engineering

Next.js · TypeScript · Stripe · PostgreSQL

unrot

Free and open source Developer tool

Catches out-of-date instructions before they mislead an AI coding assistant.

See it on GitHub↗ Install it↗

Teams now write instruction files to tell AI coding assistants how their project works. Then the project changes and the instructions don't. unrot reads those files, checks them against the actual code, and reports what no longer lines up.

The problem What came of it

An AI assistant following stale instructions confidently does the wrong thing, and nobody notices, because nothing ever checks whether those instructions are still true.

Released free and open source for anyone to install. I tested it against 69 well-known open-source projects (Microsoft's VS Code and OpenAI's Codex among them) where it found instructions pointing at files that had been deleted or renamed. In testing it catches 99% of the problems it's designed to find.

Creator and maintainer

Clipr.AI

In development AI

See a haircut on yourself before anyone picks up the scissors.

Code kept private

Clipr scans your hair from a few angles, shows you what a new cut would actually look like on you, and turns your choice into clear instructions: either a card to hand your barber or step-by-step guidance to cut it yourself.

The problem What came of it

Describing the haircut you want is genuinely hard. A photo of someone else rarely translates to your hair, and "a bit shorter on the sides" means something different to every barber.

A working app that takes you from a scan to a preview to instructions either you or your barber can follow, then compares the real result against what you asked for.

Kivo

Ready to launch Marketplace

Rent sports gear from people nearby, by the hour.

Code kept private

Kivo turns idle equipment into something rentable. Find gear near you on a map, book it, confirm the handover in person with a code, play, and pay only for the time you actually used. One account both rents and lends.

The problem What came of it

Trying a new sport means buying equipment you might use twice, while the gear other people already own sits unused in their garages.

A complete marketplace: map search, hourly booking, in-person handover codes, automatic payment for the exact time used, and payouts to owners. Built and tested end to end.
